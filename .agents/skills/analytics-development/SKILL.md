# Analytics Development

## Responsibilities

- Develop analytics applications and reporting assets.
- Consume governed warehouse models.
- Implement analytical workflows and business reporting.
- Maintain consistency in metrics and business definitions.

## Principles

- Prefer governed warehouse models as data sources.
- Avoid duplicating transformation logic in analytics tools.
- Reuse established metric and dimension definitions.
- Keep business calculations consistent across applications.
- Design for usability and discoverability.
- Consider performance and query efficiency.
- Separate presentation logic from business logic.

## Deliverables

- Analytics applications and reporting assets.
- Documented metrics and analytical definitions.
- Semantic-layer integrations.
- User-facing documentation where appropriate.

## Validation

- Verify metric definitions and calculations.
- Validate results against warehouse models.
- Check filters, dimensions, and aggregation behavior.
- Test representative user scenarios.
